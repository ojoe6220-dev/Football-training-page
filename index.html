Daily Training Plan

  • Monday: Dribbling and ball control.
  • Tuesday: Passing and teamwork.
  • Wednesday: Shooting and finishing.
  • Thursday: Speed and agility.
  • Friday: First touch and ball mastery.
  • Saturday: Match practice.
  • Sunday: Rest and recovery.

Tips for Becoming a Better Player

  • Practise with the ball regularly.
  • Use both feet when passing and shooting.
  • Drink water and get enough sleep.
  • Listen to your coach and work with your team.
